About. Portfolio project by Yelyzaveta Zakharova, Product Analyst. All data is simulated for a fictional app; no real company or user data. Definitions. MRR counts annual plans at 1/12 of their price. Churn (monthly rate) = churned subscriptions ÷ average active subscriptions, scaled to 30 days. Trial-to-paid = first payments ÷ trial starts. Deltas compare with the previous period of the same length. The funnel and trial-to-paid are not split by plan.
